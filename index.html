Notes

How I got here

A note to whoever's reading

I was born and raised in Istanbul, and I trained to fix aeroplanes. I studied avionics engineering at Atılım University, did my internships in hangars, and once helped take a retired Bombardier apart, piece by piece.

After graduating in 2018 I applied to become a pilot at Turkish Airlines. They said no. So I did my military service as a sergeant, and then I went after the thing I'd wanted for a long time: making software.

My first apps were a calisthenics tracker and a Pomodoro timer, both just for me. Around the same time I ran a small music-gear shop, where I designed and patented a guitar arm-protection band, and before all that I'd spent years translating on the side.

In 2021 an iOS bootcamp run with Hepsiburada turned into a job at Hepsiburada. Then came MigrosOne, where I worked on a fintech app with about a million daily users.

Along the way I started SwiftBuddies, an iOS meetup in Istanbul. Now I live in Berlin, and SwiftBuddies came with me.

These days I build my own apps for Mac and iPhone. Small and careful, the way I'd want to use them.

— Doğukaan
